IAU scientific parameters and consolidated input collection

Scientific origin: Archinal, B. A., Acton, C. H., A'Hearn, M. F., Conrad,
A., Consolmagno, G. J., Duxbury, T., Hestroffer, D., Hilton, J. L., Kirk,
R. L., Klioner, S. A., McCarthy, D., Meech, K., Oberst, J., Ping, J.,
Seidelmann, P. K., Tholen, D. J., Thomas, P. C., and Williams, I. P. (2018),
Report of the IAU Working Group on Cartographic Coordinates and Rotational
Elements: 2015, Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy 130:22,
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10569-017-9805-5.

Input collection: csvForWKT, PlanetMap; Jean-Christophe Malapert (CNES),
Trent Hare (USGS) and Benoit Seignovert (University of Nantes), as identified
in the collection's AUTHORS.rst. PROJ's contemporaneous issue2601 links the
thareUSGS/csvForWKT fork. The consolidated CSV currently published by
PlanetMap at commit7f693aa1d980d5e3f33b0dd94cd811f4aab3f832 has exactly the same
182 records and all field values as the PROJ9.8.1 input (line endings differ).
https://github.com/PlanetMap/csvForWKT/tree/7f693aa1d980d5e3f33b0dd94cd811f4aab3f832

PROJ's MIT generator by Even Rouault / Hobu Inc (2021) selects97 bodies,
constructs115 ellipsoids and127 geodetic CRS plus2074 projected CRS. It
converts radii/axes into sphere/biaxial definitions, sometimes computes an
arithmetic mean, assigns identifiers and longitude conventions, and excludes
unusable/unsupported definitions. It retains the report DOI. Mastixa ships
that upstream database unchanged and makes no further database edits.

Report prose, tables as typeset, illustrations, original observations, NAIF
kernels and the planetary naming database are not distributed. Scientific
values, body identifiers and short landmark names are factual inputs. Mixed
authorship is credited; no claim that the whole collection or article is a
US government work or public domain is made.

One separate legacy input is PROJ:EARTH2000: Earth ellipsoid axes6378140m and
6356750m, from the older IAU2000 dictionary. This is an isolated numeric pair
in PROJ's independent definition, not redistribution of that older dictionary.
